## Avatar and banner portrait

The portrait is an AI-generated, stylized transformation of **Elon Musk (cropped)** by **Debbie Rowe / The Royal Society**, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Elon_Musk_(cropped).jpg). Changes: reinterpreted as a minimal 2D capsule-eye character, tilted and cropped for an avatar, then embedded in the banner.
